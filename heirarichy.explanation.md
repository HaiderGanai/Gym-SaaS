Organization is the root. Every gym belongs to one organization via GYM.organization_id. Every staff user belongs to one organization via STAFF_USER.organization_id. VAT summaries and org-level reports also hang off organization directly.

Gym is the operational unit. Almost everything else connects to a gym:

Plans, discounts, slot templates, slots, invoices, subscriptions, notification logs, AI reports, and VAT summaries all carry a direct gym_id
Gym does not directly connect to members or staff anymore — those go through the access junction tables


StaffUser ↔ Gym is many-to-many through StaffGymAccess. One staff user can have access to multiple gyms. One gym can have multiple staff. Access has its own lifecycle — is_active, granted_by, revoked_at. The granted_by field is a self-referencing FK back to StaffUser — a manager grants access to a front desk employee. org_owner and org_admin roles bypass the junction entirely at the guard level — they don't need rows in StaffGymAccess.

Member ↔ Gym is many-to-many through MemberGymAccess. A member registers at one gym — that row gets is_primary = true. If granted access to another branch, a second row is added with is_primary = false. The granted_by FK points to the StaffUser who approved the access — null if the member self-registered. Member itself carries no gym_id — gym context is always resolved through this junction.

Waiver connects to both Member and Gym directly with a unique constraint on (member_id, gym_id). A member signs a fresh waiver for each branch they join. It is not shared across branches.

MembershipPlan belongs to a Gym. A plan is branch-specific — Gulberg branch can have different pricing than DHA branch even within the same organization. Plans connect downward to MemberSubscription.

Discount belongs to a Gym. No direct relation to plans or subscriptions in the schema — discount codes are validated at subscription creation time in the service layer, not enforced by a FK. If you want to track which discount was applied to which subscription, you'd need a discount_id FK on MemberSubscription — that's currently missing and worth adding before you start coding.

MemberSubscription connects Member to MembershipPlan and carries its own gym_id directly. This is intentional — even though member-gym relationship is now a junction, the subscription must be pinned to a specific branch because the plan belongs to that branch. It generates Invoice records — one subscription has many invoices over its lifetime.

Invoice connects to Member, MemberSubscription, and Gym directly. It snapshots vat_number and invoice_number at creation time — these fields are immutable after creation. The tax fields (tax_amount, tax_rate, net_amount) are calculated by VatService at invoice creation and also frozen. Invoice status drives the dunning queue.

VatPeriodSummary connects to both Gym and Organization. The gym-level row is the source of truth — aggregated from paid invoices in that period. The organization sees across all its gyms by querying all VatPeriodSummary rows under its organization_id. There is no separate org-level VAT entity — the rollup is a query, not a stored record.

SlotTemplate belongs to Gym and references a StaffUser as the default instructor. It carries an RRULE string — the template itself has no time instances, just the recurrence rule.

Slot belongs to Gym, references SlotTemplate as its origin (nullable — a slot can be created manually without a template), and references a StaffUser as instructor which can differ from the template's default. Slot is the actual bookable time instance.

Booking connects Member to Slot. No direct gym_id on booking — gym context is derived through slot.gym_id. Booking has a self-managed waitlist via waitlist_position integer. The qr_token is a signed JWT containing booking_id + member_id + slot_id — verified at check-in without a DB lookup.

NotificationLog connects to Gym and optionally to Member. Member is nullable because some notifications are gym-wide broadcasts with no single recipient — staff announcements, for example.

AiReport belongs to Gym. One report per gym per day. Generated by aggregating that gym's bookings, check-ins, revenue, and no-shows then passed to Gemini.

OrgReport belongs to Organization. Generated monthly. It does not FK into AiReport — it independently aggregates from raw tables across all gyms under the org. The metrics_by_gym jsonb field embeds a snapshot of each gym's numbers at report generation time.

The one missing link called out earlier: Discount has no FK on MemberSubscription. Right now you can't query "how many times was promo code SUMMER20 used" or "which members got a discount." Add discount_id uuid FK → Discount nullable on MemberSubscription before you start that module.
